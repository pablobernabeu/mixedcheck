## Claims checked

1. The composition of mixor's SmokingPrevention data (flay-1995.md), which the trial's papers do not describe.
   **Settled from a published description.** Section 3 analyses the Television, School and Family Smoking Prevention and Cessation Project: 7th-grade students pretested in January 1986 and post-tested in April 1986, in 28 Los Angeles schools randomised at the school level to the classroom curriculum, the television intervention, both, or a no-treatment control, a 2 × 2 of curriculum by television. Students were kept if they had complete scores on the tobacco and health knowledge scale at both tests, which gave 1,600 students in 135 classrooms and 28 schools, with 1 to 13 classrooms per school and 2 to 28 students per classroom. The post-test score is grouped into four ordered categories (0–1, 2, 3 and 4–7 correct), with the pre-test score as a covariate. These are the size, site, design and variables that mixor's documentation gives for SmokingPrevention, so the data hold 28 schools and 135 classrooms, seven schools per arm if the arms are those of Flay et al. (1995, Figure 1), and no attention-control school. Section 6 says that the datasets are available from the author on request; the article does not name mixor.

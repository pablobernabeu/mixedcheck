# AMPPS: article processing charge

**Pages read.** Association for Psychological Science, AMPPS submission guidelines (https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/ampps/ampps-submission-guidelines), page marked 'Updated 10/29/26', read 21 November 2026; web search results of the same day showing the Sage and DOAJ listings for the journal.

**Fact.** AMPPS is fully open access, and authors are charged an article processing charge of $1,000 after acceptance; the guidelines add that this does not reflect APS's production costs, which are substantially higher and which APS subsidises. APC and waiver requests are handled by production staff after acceptance, not by the editors. The Sage and DOAJ listings found by the search give a higher list price (up to $2,000, with a discount to $1,000 since the journal moved to open access).

**For the record.** open_items_local_session.csv records conflicting figures of $1,000 and $1,500. The journal's own current guidelines give $1,000. Whether Oxford's agreements with Sage cover it is a separate question (see the note on Oxford's open-access coverage).

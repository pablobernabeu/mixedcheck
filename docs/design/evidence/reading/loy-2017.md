## Claims checked

1. open_items_local_session.csv: 'Lineup protocol for LMM diagnostics (catalogue L5-14)'; review-catalogue.md, L5-14: null panels come from a parametric bootstrap of the fitted model with refitting, and the same residual type is recomputed on each (Loy, Hofmann & Cook 2017).
   **Confirmed.** The protocol (Section 6, step 1) simulates new responses from the proposed model (for diagnostics) or the reduced model (for selection) by parametric bootstrap, refits the model to each simulated response and extracts the same residuals, giving 19 null datasets for a lineup of 20. Section 5 applies this to level-1 residuals and to predicted random slopes, and Section 4 to model selection, where the null plots come from a model without the variable of interest.
2. Further points of the protocol, for L5-14: the observed plot is placed at random among the nulls; independent observers pick the most different plot and give the feature that led to the choice; the strength of evidence is assessed with the visual distribution of Hofmann et al. (2015); in routine model building the modeller looks at lineups blind to the position of the true plot, and independent observers are recruited only for critical decisions (Section 6). The study itself used 75 lineups and 4,927 evaluations by 487 observers recruited online.
3. Not in this paper: the catalogue's second condition, that the observer should not see the data plot before the lineup, is attributed there to a 2024 paper (JCGS 33, 1497–1511), not to Loy et al. (2017). Section 6 is consistent with it (the modeller is blinded to the true plot).

## Claims checked

1. review-catalogue.md (L4 entry on information criteria) and reports.json (research:re-structure, recommendation 3): the marginal AIC is not an asymptotically unbiased estimator of the Akaike information and favours smaller models without random effects; the conditional AIC that ignores the estimation uncertainty in the random-effects covariance matrix is biased towards keeping any random effect not predicted to be exactly zero; the authors derive a corrected conditional AIC. **Confirmed** (abstract). The abstract adds that the corrected conditional AIC has an analytic form that avoids costly and imprecise numerical approximations, and that it is implemented in an R package.
2. reports.json (research:re-structure, open item): the size of the marginal AIC's bias. **Not checked**: this needs the full text, which was not readable here.
3. The correction. It replaces Tables 2 and 3 of the paper's Supplementary Material, which contained errors; the article itself is unchanged (correction notice). Any use of those supplementary tables should take the corrected version.

## Claims checked

1. open_items_local_session.csv: 'Cholesky residuals; residual checks in V1/V2'.
   **Supported.** The paper proposes multiplying the estimated marginal residual vector by the Cholesky factor of the inverse of the estimated marginal variance matrix; the resulting rotated residuals are used to build an empirical distribution function with pointwise standard errors as a graphical check of normal errors (abstract, p. 383). The authors report that the method works for clustered data with independent sampling units and for models in which all observations are correlated, such as a single time series, and that it can give satisfactory results when not all the technical conditions of their theory hold (abstract). The keywords name linear mixed models and random effects.

## Claims checked

1. open_items_local_session.csv: 'Standardised LMM residuals; V2 modules M01-M03'. **Supported.** The paper reviews the three kinds of residual of a linear mixed model (marginal, conditional and predicted random effects) and proposes a standardisation of the conditional residual for finding outlying observations and clusters (abstract, p. 863).
2. review-catalogue.md (L5 entry on conditional residuals): standardise conditional residuals as ê / sqrt(σ̂⁴ Pᵢᵢ), 'extended for leverage as in Nobre & Singer 2007'; the verifier notes that the extension is redundant, since σ²P = I − H already holds the fixed- and random-effect leverage. **Supported, and the verifier's point stands.** Equation 2.4 (p. 865) divides each conditional residual by σ̂ times the square root of the corresponding diagonal element of ΣQΣ, and the authors note that these elements are functions of the joint leverage of the fixed and random effects, so that the standardised residual generalises the usual studentised residual. The paper's standardisation is the leverage-based one itself, not an extension of another.
3. The correction. It replaces an incorrect equation in the paper's worked example (the reduced model 3.3) and adds its missing number (correction notice, p. 875). It does not affect the residuals.

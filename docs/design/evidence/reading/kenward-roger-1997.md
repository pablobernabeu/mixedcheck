## Claims checked

1. review-catalogue.md (L4 entry on denominator degrees of freedom): Kenward–Roger changes both the df and the covariance matrix of the fixed effects, using an adjusted covariance estimator with smaller small-sample bias, and reproduces exact F tests where they exist.
   **Confirmed** (Summary, p. 983). The paper presents a scaled Wald statistic with an F approximation to its distribution; the statistic uses an adjusted estimator of the covariance matrix with reduced small-sample bias; and the approach reproduces both the statistics and the F distributions where these are exact, namely Hotelling T²-type statistics and ANOVA F ratios. Its performance is assessed in simulations of four REML analyses and illustrated with three examples. The paper is framed for REML fits (title and Summary), which agrees with the catalogue's point that Kenward–Roger needs REML.
2. open_items_local_session.csv: 'Denominator df comparator in V1'. **Supported** as a description of the method's role; the paper's settings are data made of independent sets with a block-diagonal covariance matrix (Section 1, p. 983), the nested and longitudinal designs that V1 covers.

# Oxford's open-access agreements: Springer Nature, APA and Sage

**Pages read.** Bodleian Libraries, 'Publisher deals' (https://www.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/open-research/open-access-publishing/journal-article/publisher-deals) and 'Article processing charges (APCs)' (https://www.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/open-research/open-access-publishing/apc), read 21 November 2026. The page gives no date of last update. The Sage list of participating journals that it links (uk.sagepub.com, 'uk-jisc-agreement') returned 'page not found' on the same day.

**Fact.** Oxford pays for open access only through publisher deals or block grants, and does not reimburse APCs paid individually. To use a deal, an author must be publishing a journal article, be its corresponding author and submit with an Oxford email address; the academic division then pays. On 21 November 2026 the page listed:

- **Springer Nature**: Oxford corresponding authors may publish fully open access in most hybrid Springer, Nature and Palgrave journals; articles in fully open-access journals get a 15% discount.
- **American Psychological Association (APA) / Hogrefe Collection**: research articles in 87 hybrid APA titles and 15 hybrid Hogrefe titles; fully open-access Hogrefe journals get a 25% discount.
- **Sage** (a Jisc deal): unlimited open access for research and review articles in most Sage hybrid ('SAGE Choice') journals, with some exclusions, and a 20% discount in Sage's fully open-access journals.

The page lists no deal with the Association for Psychological Science. (An entry offering a free 'APS membership' belongs to the American Physiological Society.)

**For the record.** Behavior Research Methods is a hybrid Springer journal and Psychological Methods a hybrid APA journal, so both would normally fall under these deals, but the page does not list individual titles for Springer Nature or APA. AMPPS is a fully open-access journal published by Sage for APS; if it counts among Sage's fully open-access titles, the 20% discount would apply to its $1,000 APC, but the Sage list could not be checked. open_items_local_session.csv also asks about the Springer Nature–Jisc agreement of 2026–2028 and the APA arrangement after July 2026: the page shows both publishers' deals as current but gives no dates.

**Also asked by the CSV: the JORS APC.** The Journal of Open Research Software's 'Submission Guidelines' (https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/about/submissions, read 21 November 2026) give, under 'Publication Fees', an APC of £824.00 for software metapapers and £891 for the section 'Issues in Research Software', payable on acceptance ('About', https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/about). Whether an Oxford deal covers JORS was not checked.

# Scope wording of the candidate journals

**Pages read, 21 November 2026.** Behavior Research Methods, 'Submission guidelines', General Information (https://link.springer.com/journal/13428/submission-guidelines); Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science, 'Submission Guidelines' (https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/ampps/ampps-submission-guidelines, updated 29 October 2026); Psychological Methods, journal home page (https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/met); Journal of Statistical Software, 'Information on Mission' (see jss-scope.md).

**Fact.**
- **Behavior Research Methods** publishes articles on the methods, techniques and instrumentation of research in experimental psychology, focusing on computer technology in psychological research, stimulus development and practical data analysis, and asks authors to make materials and code easy to reuse. It accepts original research articles and tutorials, the latter including statistical techniques and their application and reproducible design.
- **AMPPS** brings methodological advances to non-methodologists across psychology. Its Research Practices section takes best-practice articles, statistics tutorials, articles on research tools, commentaries, simulation studies of new techniques and debates; its Empirical section takes methodological articles, large-scale and multi-lab studies and registered reports. Papers must interest researchers in many areas of psychology, and AMPPS does not publish methods papers relevant mainly to one task or subfield.
- **Psychological Methods** is devoted to the development and dissemination of methods for collecting, analysing, understanding and interpreting psychological data: innovations in design, measurement, methodology and quantitative and qualitative analysis, for an audience that includes method developers, teachers of quantitative methods and users. It solicits original theoretical, quantitative, empirical and methodological articles, reviews of methodological issues, tutorials, applications of new procedures and articles on teaching quantitative methods.
- **JSS** publishes open-source software with reproducible articles and not methodological innovations as such (jss-scope.md).

**For the record.** 02-decisions.md (Q9) sends the ladder study's Stage 1 report to BRM or AMPPS, the validation paper to BRM, and reserves Psychological Methods for a reframed general comparison of diagnostic procedures. The scopes support these placements: AMPPS asks for broad relevance across psychology, which a study of random-effects reduction has; BRM's scope is data analysis in experimental psychology; Psychological Methods takes methodological articles generally and now also registered reports (rr-psychological-methods.md).

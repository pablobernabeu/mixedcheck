**Fact.**
- Data diagnostics: describe the planned diagnostics, including criteria for excluding participants after data collection, criteria and methods for imputing missing data, the definition and handling of statistical outliers, analyses of data distributions and any transformations.
- Analytic strategy: describe the strategy for inferential statistics and the protection against experiment-wise error, for primary, secondary and exploratory hypotheses.
- Statistics and data analysis: report the extent of missing data, the evidence or arguments for its mechanism (MCAR, MAR or MNAR) and the methods used for it; for each primary and secondary outcome, the number of cases, cell means, standard deviations and other descriptive measures for the total sample and each subgroup; and inferential results with exact p values where null-hypothesis tests are used and the minimal statistics needed to reconstruct them (for example df and mean squares), with effect sizes.
- The correction concerns only a citation in the section on clinical trials (p. 13): the Declaration of Helsinki should be the 2008 version. It does not change Table 1.

**Bearing on Q5 (02-decisions.md).** The record says JARS-Quant 'informs the minimum content, pending a full reading of its tables'. Table 1's data-diagnostics and analytic-strategy rows map onto what the suite's methods text and preregistration block can generate from the declaration and the plan. Its descriptive row asks for cell means and standard deviations per subgroup, which a shareable report can give only within the privacy filter's rules (k = 10 members per cell and the dominance rule); df and test statistics, which JARS asks for exactly, are among the values the filter shows exactly. Missing-data reporting (extent and mechanism) is outcome-derived and so falls under the tier-A guarantee.

# Psychological Methods: Registered Reports of simulation studies

**Page read.** American Psychological Association, 'Call for papers: Introducing Registered Reports at Psychological Methods' (https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/met/introducing-registered-reports), read 21 November 2026.

**Fact.** Psychological Methods is introducing registered reports for methodological work through a special issue edited by J. K. Flake and D. B. McCoach. Stage 1 manuscripts are due on 1 April 2027, submitted through Editorial Manager under the article type 'Special Issue: Registered Report', with first decisions expected by the end of May 2027. The journal does not review proposals or letters of intent, but questions about fit can go to the guest editor. A Stage 1 manuscript gives the introduction, the proposed methods and a complete analysis plan, with enough detail for reviewers to check the data-generating and analysis code and reproduce mock results. For a simulation study it gives the data-generating mechanisms, the methods compared, the performance measures and the number of repetitions, and any pilot or preliminary simulations and how they shaped the design; a limited number of replications or conditions can show that the code runs before the full run. Stage 2 is judged on adherence to the approved plan, with deviations and exploratory analyses reported. Registered reports need not be simulation studies. The call lists Siepe et al. (2024) and Pawel et al. (2024) among its motivations and recommends resources including a 2026 preprint by Siepe and colleagues on whether and how to preregister a simulation study.

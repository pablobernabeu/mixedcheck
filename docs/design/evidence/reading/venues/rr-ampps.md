# AMPPS: Registered Reports of simulation studies

**Page read.** Association for Psychological Science, 'Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science Submission Guidelines' (https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/ampps/ampps-submission-guidelines), page marked 'Updated 10/29/26', read 21 November 2026, sections on the journal's scope, the types of articles, 'Registered Reports and Registered Replication Reports' and the PCI RR partnership.

**Fact.** AMPPS considers single-lab or collaborative registered reports focusing primarily on methodological issues that are relevant to several areas of psychology or of broad practical importance; it does not take single-lab empirical articles on theoretical questions within one literature, or method papers relevant mainly to one task or subfield. Registered reports are reviewed before data collection, for whether the planned study is well motivated and designed and would be meaningful whatever the outcome. Simulation studies appear in the guidelines among the Research Practices section's articles ('simulation studies for new techniques') and as a form of General Article, which are submitted as finished work; the guidelines do not name simulation studies as a registered-report type in so many words. AMPPS is 'PCI RR-friendly': it automatically offers Stage 1 in-principle acceptance to any quantitative Stage 1 registered report within its scope that has received in-principle acceptance at Peer Community in Registered Reports, and accepts without further review a Stage 2 report recommended by PCI RR, subject to the journal's requirements.

**For the record.** 02-decisions.md (Q9) says the ADEMP-PreReg authors list AMPPS as accepting registered reports of simulation studies; their template's wiki does so (see the note on Siepe et al., 2024). The journal's own wording supports methodological registered reports in general. The PCI RR route is an alternative way to in-principle acceptance at AMPPS.

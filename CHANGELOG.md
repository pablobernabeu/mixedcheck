# Changelog

Notable changes to the design of mixedcheck and to its shared specification are recorded here, in the format of [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Changes to the packages are recorded in [r/NEWS.md](r/NEWS.md) and [python/CHANGELOG.md](python/CHANGELOG.md).

## Unreleased

### Added

- The [design review](docs/design/01-design-review.md) tests the original two-stream architecture against statsmodels 0.15.0 and the sources of lme4, glmmTMB, performance and insight, lists the edge cases with the greatest consequences and closes with ten questions. The [review catalogue](docs/design/evidence/review-catalogue.md) keeps the 152 verified items behind it.
- The [decision record](docs/design/02-decisions.md) answers the ten questions, marks each answer and any part still open as 'Decided', 'Provisional' or 'Maintainer' and defines the conventions shared by both packages: six status fields, a hash taxonomy with keyed commitments for person-level data, two JSON profiles and the handling of UTF-8 and NFC.
- The [evidence for the decisions](docs/design/evidence/decisions/README.md) is the frozen record of the November 2026 research reports, their verifiers and a consistency critic: a 27-model engine benchmark, simulations, declaration schemas, a pathology-zoo prototype, SLURM templates and a cost model.
- The [execution plan](docs/design/03-execution-plan.md) turns the decisions into gated milestones, from the repository skeleton (M0) to GLMM support (M8), with validation on ARC, risks and open checks.
- The repository skeleton of milestone M0. The R package in r/ and the Python package in python/ return the versions of the shared specification through `spec_versions()`. spec/ holds those versions and the list of shared artefacts, which spec/tools/vendor.py copies byte for byte into both packages and checks against their sources. GitHub Actions check both packages and the vendored copies, and the repository gains governance files, a citation file and a [provenance log](docs/provenance.md).
- The [conventions](docs/spec/conventions.md) of milestone M1 specify the strict JSON reader, the hashable and data profiles, RFC 8785 serialisation with ECMAScript number formatting, SHA-256 hashes, HMAC-SHA-256 keyed commitments, the Unicode policy and the six status fields, which together make up canonicaliser version 0.1.0. spec/errors.json, described in [errors.md](docs/spec/errors.md), is the catalogue of error and warning codes that both packages share, and spec/schemas/ holds schema bundle 0.1.0.
- Declaration vocabulary 0.1 ([declarations.md](docs/spec/declarations.md)) applies the corrections of Q4 to the draft schema. It has eight blocks, rejects unknown keys everywhere, including the deferred dyad, EEG and imputation blocks, and replaces the draft's unkeyed data hash with a keyed commitment. declarations.md also sets out the rules that validators enforce in code, the order of the checks and the conversion of YAML to JSON.
- Registry 0.1.0 ([registry.md](docs/spec/registry.md)) holds 24 checks: the T0 audits that M2 implements, four T1 checks that read the outcome by grouping factor and the exploratory T2 separation check. Every threshold is descriptive, and every literature source is marked as not read in full until the local session. The entries are written in YAML under spec/registry/ and compiled by spec/tools/compile_registry.py into spec/compiled/registry.json.
- [documents.md](docs/spec/documents.md) specifies the results document, with its access log and internal sidecar, and drafts at version 0.1 of the plan and the bring-your-own estimates contract, which M3 and later milestones refine. A results document records each finding with its six status fields and the counts that the privacy filter needs, the versions and hashes of everything the run rests on and keyed commitments to the data.
- The canonical IR of a model ([ir.md](docs/spec/ir.md)) holds what both packages need to build X and Z identically. ir.md defines the lme4-style formula grammar as an EBNF, term expansion in the order R and lme4 use, contrasts with explicit reference levels, level order, centring, the design and analysis samples and the sequential rule for dropping aliased columns.
- [tables.md](docs/spec/tables.md) specifies the table reader of M2: the CSV text it accepts, missing values, the Frictionless lexical form of each field type and the primary and unique keys. spec/unicode/assigned-14.0.json lists the code points that Unicode 14.0 assigns, so that both packages normalise the same strings.
- The [conformance corpora](fixtures/README.md) hold 907 cases for the canonicaliser (the strict reader, serialisation, numbers, Unicode, SHA-256 and HMAC-SHA-256), 192 declarations in JSON and 84 in YAML, and 192 table cases and 211 formula cases, which M2a raises to 194 and 250. Their expected outputs come from the specifications and from independent oracles: the RFC vectors, the Python packages rfc8785 and jsonschema, Python's hashlib, hmac and unicodedata modules, R's yaml package and Node.js.
- The [parity harness](parity/README.md) runs both packages over every corpus, R under C.UTF-8 and C, and compares each outcome with the other language and with the expected outcome. It runs on pull requests that touch either package, spec/, fixtures/ or parity/. At the end of M1, the 1,183 cases of the corpora that M1 implements agreed in both languages and with the corpora, and the 403 table and formula cases waited for M2.
- The workflow references.yaml checks the metadata of every reference with a DOI in docs/ against Crossref. It runs when started by hand or when the label 'references' is added to a pull request, because the cloud sessions cannot reach Crossref.
- The [privacy filter](docs/spec/privacy.md) specifies how a shareable results document is made from the findings of a run, and the M2 section of the [execution plan](docs/design/03-execution-plan.md) specifies `audit()`, `write_results()`, `validate_results()` and the results object. The [audit and results corpora](fixtures/README.md) hold 265 and 142 cases written from the specifications, with structure tests in spec/tools/.

### Changed

- The maintainer chose mixedcheck as the name of both packages, with lmecheck as the fallback, subject to the registry checks, which run as a GitHub Actions job. The maintainer holds the copyright. Once M0 is merged and the name checks pass, the repository is renamed and made public. The privacy defaults of Q5 are adopted, to be checked against the UK output-checking guidance before 1.0, and the paper route is settled at P1.
- The H0 pilot runs on ARC before the M3b gate, with its Apptainer image built there from a rocker image and a dated package snapshot. One local session at the start of M3b covers the pilot, the full texts, the venue facts and the privacy guidance, and checks that need only network access run in GitHub Actions.
- Following the consistency critic, the Stream B plan object carries a language-neutral design-plan hash and an engine-specific execution binding, declares a terminal state for a failed protected rung and builds each reduction step from encodings that every 1.0 engine can express. R gains cli and utf8 as Imports. The execution plan builds each component in R first and places the R convergence-triage core (M3b) before the Stage 1 Registered Report.
- The roadmap returns lognormal models to version 1.2 and makes zero-inflated and hurdle models candidates for version 2. The separation check runs in M2 as an exploratory T2 audit, and the shared view model and privacy filter move into M2. V7 includes an engine conformance study whose regenerated parity tiers gate 1.0.
- A rung that an engine cannot express is reported as not_available with its reason. The unprotected back-off is available only by explicit opt-in, and the first 0.x release ships neither it nor protected AIC. Point-level detail appears only in internal mode. Only a Rust-based engine can be promoted to 'supported' and become the default for new Python fits, and gpboost may serve only as an engine-specific adapter.
- Stream B parity is exact except for the per-coefficient bounds of the separation check, which are compared within tolerances stored in the registry. Rung 0 may contain terms that an engine cannot express, and the statsmodels adapter also covers uncorrelated crossed slopes. For Gaussian LMMs, masking cannot change the rung an automated rule selects, so masked execution is an attestation. R Suggests gains lpSolve and the packages whose datasets the R examples use. The execution plan defers the families of version 1.2 and the structured covariance terms of version 1.3, and a validator for vocabulary 0.1 rejects the deferred declaration blocks as unknown keys.
- Most topics are now stated fully in one document and referred to from the others. The decision record owns the decisions and their evidence. The execution plan owns sequencing, environments, risks and open checks, and the evidence README describes the evidence workflow.
- The execution plan declares each R Import when the code first uses it and each Python extra with its first optional dependency. In place of the LF line endings of Q7, Git's line-ending conversion is turned off for the shared artefacts, so that fixtures can keep deliberate CRLF cases, and the vendoring check rejects CR characters in spec/ sources. The nightly full matrix runs only once the repository is public.
- The design documents, the READMEs and the review catalogue were revised for plain British English, with paragraphs regrouped by topic and status values written as their defined identifiers. The root README describes packages for mixed-effects models in general and states that the MIT licence excludes the third-party material identified in the evidence. The review catalogue gives its paths into the ephemeral review environment as 'scratchpad/' paths and states that those scripts were not kept.
- With the table reader, the formula parser and the design matrices of M2a, [tables.md](docs/spec/tables.md) (Keys) states that the primary key is checked record by record, with two new table cases (194 in all), and [ir.md](docs/spec/ir.md) settles the points that M2a and its review found open. Among them are a subtracted random term, the design sample of centring constants, limits of 10,000 terms, columns and characters, levels that the IR cannot hold, values that are not finite and unambiguous labels of grouping levels, with the codes E6038 and E6074 to E6077 and 39 new formula cases (250 in all). The M2 section of the [execution plan](docs/design/03-execution-plan.md) records these and the other decisions of M2a.
- Two reviews settled 123 points of [registry 0.1.0](docs/spec/registry.md) and of the specifications of M2 where implementers could differ or a shareable document could give back a suppressed count, with the entries kept at version 1 and the solver of outcome.separation in R now lpSolve. A run refuses empty samples, data without a declared column and unknown families (E7007, E5011 and E7008), and the entry schema gains quantiles, the resolution of by_unit counts, suppression with other statistics and parts of a total, message fallbacks and nouns (E4022 to E4024). [documents.md](docs/spec/documents.md) defines the canonical analysis table, the file suffix of internal mode and the banners, and [ir.md](docs/spec/ir.md) adds E6078 for events and trials that are not counts.
- The [parity harness](parity/README.md) also runs the table and formula corpora and compares the columns read, the samples, the IR bytes and hashes and the design matrices bit for bit. At the end of M2a, all 1,627 cases of the corpora agree in both languages and with their expected outcomes.
- While the R package implemented the audits and the privacy filter, independent oracles raised points that the specification now settles, with the entries of [registry 0.1.0](docs/spec/registry.md) kept at version 1. outcome.separation gains the parameter residue_tolerance, so that values of u between -1e-11 and 0, which are mostly rounding residues of lpSolve, no longer make a finding near_threshold. assignment.design_df subtracts the first row of each level instead of the level's mean, whose rounding left residues that the sequential rule kept. The icc of structure.within_between is 'NaN' where its absence would show the numbers of levels and rows. [privacy.md](docs/spec/privacy.md) names the items whose levels the rules across findings compare, treats the rows of a pair of columns of structure.label_incidence and structure.id_scope_evidence as a sample, as it treats the rows of an assignment, exempts from the rule on equal values every count that resolves as non-person, by_unit counts included, and makes part_of independent of the order of the rules. The audit corpus gains a case with a class-level covariate whose class means are inexact and a case of an absent ratio written as suppressed.
- A review of the R implementation settled further points. A Stream B run also refuses the response and its trials column with E6069, and checks the sample columns, units and assignments for them ([registry.md](docs/spec/registry.md), Tier and inputs). The [privacy filter](docs/spec/privacy.md) compares every two nested samples, carries the loss of the combinations of crossed columns to the other sample, treats the contributors of the basis grouping_factors as levels and suppresses minima and maxima as quantiles. The icc of structure.within_between is internal_only, effective_size of structure.cluster_sizes is suppressed with the rows, structure.id_scope_evidence gains the statistic levels and E7024 covers a by_unit ratio of class person whose numerator or denominator is left out. assignment.design_df and identifiability.df_budget, now defined through the same X_d, set to 0 a column of X_d that is constant within the levels up to rounding, and identifiability.gram_rank, design.collinearity and design.scale_disparity divide each column by its largest absolute value before forming products, so that extreme magnitudes neither overflow nor underflow. identifiability.gram_rank gains the parameter max_parameters, and [conventions.md](docs/spec/conventions.md) compares condition_number of design.scale_disparity within a tolerance that grows with its value. The audit corpus gains two cases and the results corpus one, and spec/tools/rehash_results.py updates the hashes of the results corpus after a change to the registry.
- In R, outcome.separation solves each linear program through its dual, which has one constraint for each coefficient, and solves the program itself only when the dual has no verified solution ([outcome.separation.yaml](spec/registry/checks/outcome.separation.yaml)), since the formulation of the build reported no optimum on 30 of 84 generated tables on which HiGHS gives a verdict. The [privacy filter](docs/spec/privacy.md) suppresses its finding as a whole for the class binomial when the rows without events, without non-events or with both number fewer than min_count, and when the design sample has at most two kept columns. Its number of kept columns is internal_only, and `software.packages` names the solver whenever the check is available.
- In R, where /dev/urandom does not exist, as on Windows, or cannot be read, a commitment key is created with `openssl::rand_bytes()` when the suggested package openssl is installed, so that E1312 arises only without it ([conventions.md](docs/spec/conventions.md), Keys).
- The conformance corpora are vendored into the packages' test directories, r/tests/conformance/ and python/tests/conformance/, rather than installed with the packages. With the audit and results corpora, the installed R package would have exceeded the 5 MB at which R CMD check reports a NOTE ([spec/README.md](spec/README.md) and 02-decisions.md, Q7).

### Fixed

- Benchmark (Q1): the common objective reproduced lme4's LMM criteria and glmmTMB's unweighted ones to within 4e-10. It reproduced glmmTMB's Laplace values to within 1.5e-12 on two datasets but only to within 1.4e-8 on the crossed binary design. glmmTMB's weighted criteria differed by about 2,134 units and lme4 1.1-35.1's Laplace values by up to 1.6e-3. statsmodels could not represent eight of the 27 models and was 24 to 27 times slower than lme4 on crossed variance components. The verifier's 200 random starts on each of five small designs found no objective more than 1e-12 below the engines' best, where the record had claimed a confirmed global optimum.
- Validation and cost (Q9): the acceptable band of .025 to .075 rests on Bradley's liberal criterion as a whole. The small-cluster heteroscedasticity check gave false-flag rates of .07 to .18 with raw residuals, and the central Phase 1 estimate of about 1,140 CPU-hours includes about 100 for V4 and V6. Maintainer time rather than compute is the binding constraint only if the project's credit allocation on ARC is confirmed.
- Other figures and attributions: the hash-oracle test recovered a whole outcome column of 20 pupils in 1.6 s, and the zoo has nine prototyped fixtures and one matched negative. The 94-package closure is that of DHARMa with Matrix, jsonlite and lme4, and Barr (2013) is cited for the interaction slope in the protected set. The design review corrects its statements on formulaic's handling of emptied factor levels, on the precision of pandas' `to_json()`, on the statsmodels fits above the J − 1 rank bound (7 of 12, of which 4 were non-singular), on whether GLMM fits from both languages can share an objective and on how often ML refits are singular.
- Chromium has produced tagged PDFs from printed pages since version 87, and other browsers have not been checked. The demonstration package's R CMD check found no undeclared-dependency or S3-consistency problems but gave warnings and a note. The record no longer claims that the same-name precedents have separate teams, and it states that some experiments were not rerun and that many references in the evidence files remain unverified. R 4.2.2 bundles Matrix 1.5-1, which the record had inferred from release dates.
- References: Bradley (1978) gains its issue and DOI. The evidence README corrects the page range of Halekoh and Højsgaard (2014) to 1–32, confirms the entries for Seabold and Perktold (2010) and Harville (1977) and records an erratum for report A's Laplace result.
- The evidence README maps `reports.json` to the folders and identifies third-party material. It also counts the scripts with hard-coded paths in each folder and lists the material that was not kept and the byte-identical files. Report F's copy of the JOSS submission guide was removed, because its licence notice was missing.

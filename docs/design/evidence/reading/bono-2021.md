## Claims checked

03-execution-plan.md lists the paper for 'the GLMM reporting rates'. The figures used in the evidence (C/q2_model_class_matrix.csv, C/q5_standards_to_report_elements.csv and reports.json, research:scope-reports) are checked here.

1. Sample: 118 GLMM analyses from 80 empirical articles indexed in Journal Citation Reports in 2018. **Confirmed** (abstract).
2. Half of the analyses had nominal outcomes. **Confirmed**: 59 of 118 (50%) nominal, 6 (5.1%) ordinal, 43 (36.4%) interval or ratio, 10 (8.5%) counts; 87.7% of nominal or ordinal outcomes had two categories (Table 2).
3. Family table: binomial 19.5%, Poisson 9.3%, negative binomial 2.5%. **Confirmed**: binomial 23 (19.5%), Poisson 11 (9.3%), negative binomial 3 (2.5%), with Bernoulli 2 (1.7%), gamma 5 (4.2%), multinomial 2 (1.7%), normal 7 (5.9%), 'non-normal' 3 (2.5%) and not specified 62 (52.5%) (Table 2). The binomial share leaves out the two analyses labelled Bernoulli.
4. Distribution or link unstated in more than half. **Confirmed**: distribution not specified in 62 of 118 (52.5%, Table 2) and link function not specified in 72 of 118 (61%, Table 3). The estimation method was not specified in 97 (82.2%, Table 3).
5. Overdispersion mentioned in 9.3% of analyses. **Confirmed with a wording change**: the table counts analyses that reported an evaluation of overdispersion, 11 of 118 (9.3%, Table 5).
6. Covariance structure reported in 13.9%. **Correct only with its denominator**: Table 5 counts the covariance structure over the 72 analyses that were not cross-sectional, of which 62 (86.1%) did not specify it, so 10 of 72 (13.9%) did. It is not 13.9% of all 118 analyses.

# ICO: anonymisation guidance

**Pages read.** Information Commissioner's Office, 'Anonymisation' (https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-sharing/anonymisation/), its chapters 'About this guidance' and 'How do we ensure anonymisation is effective?', read 21 November 2026. Version: the guidance shows 'Latest updates – 28 March 2025', and a banner states that, because of changes made by the Data (Use and Access) Act, it is under review and may change.

**Fact.** Anonymisation should make the risk of identification sufficiently remote. Identifiability is broad (not only names) and lies on a spectrum, and is judged by the means reasonably likely to be used to identify someone, taking account of the information and of who may get or want access to it; purely hypothetical chances need not be considered, and the law does not require the risk to be reduced to zero. Information inferred about a person, such as an income inferred from a house price, can itself be personal data. Before releasing data to the world at large, robust techniques should be used, because that release carries a higher risk than controlled release to known recipients; a 'motivated intruder' test is a good starting point, considering the intruder's relationship to the person, background knowledge, targeting and the value of the information; and risk assessments should be reviewed regularly. The guidance sets no numerical thresholds for cell sizes or suppression.

**Bearing on Q5 (02-decisions.md).** The guidance supports the design of the shareable mode in principle: release beyond known recipients calls for robust protection, and inference counts. It gives no basis for the specific parameters (k = 10, dominance (2, 0.90), the quantile rule, rounding to base 5, zeros treated as disclosive), which must come from output-checking practice (the ONS, DfE and SDC Handbook rules). Its point on inferred information matches the record's tier-A guarantee for outcome-derived values. The banner on the Act's changes means the guidance should be rechecked before 1.0.

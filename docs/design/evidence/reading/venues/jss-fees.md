# JSS: fees

**Pages read.** Journal of Statistical Software, 'Information for Authors' (https://www.jstatsoft.org/authors) and 'Information on Mission', section 'Copyright and Costs' (https://www.jstatsoft.org/mission), read 21 November 2026.

**Fact.** As a matter of principle, JSS charges no author fees and no subscription fees (Information for Authors). Articles are published open access under the Creative Commons Attribution licence, with authors keeping copyright (Copyright and Costs).

**For the record.** 02-decisions.md (Q9) names JSS as the single-paper alternative to JOSS. Publishing there would cost nothing.

## Claims checked

1. spec/registry/checks/design.collinearity.yaml (thresholds moderate and high, locator Section 3.3.3): a widely quoted rule of thumb treats a variance inflation factor above 5 or 10 as a problematic amount of collinearity. **Confirmed** (p. 101): the book defines the VIF as the ratio of a coefficient's variance in the full model to its variance when fitted alone, notes that its smallest possible value is 1 (no collinearity) and that some collinearity is usual in practice, and gives, as a rule of thumb, that a VIF exceeding 5 or 10 indicates a problematic amount of collinearity. The book presents 5 and 10 as alternatives, not as two grades; the registry's use of 5 for 'moderate' and 10 for 'high' is therefore its own grading of the two values.

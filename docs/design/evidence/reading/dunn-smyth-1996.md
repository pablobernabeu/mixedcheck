## Claims checked

1. review-catalogue.md (L3 and L5 entries on residuals): randomised quantile residuals are exactly normal under the true model even for discrete outcomes, with the qualification 'apart from sampling variability in the estimated parameters'; for discrete outcomes the probability integral transform needs randomisation, so the residuals depend on the seed.
   **Confirmed** (abstract, p. 236). The paper defines residuals for regression models with independent responses by inverting the fitted distribution function at each response and taking the equivalent standard normal quantile, with randomisation for discrete distributions, and states that they are exactly normal apart from sampling variability in the estimated parameters. The definition is for independent responses; its use for the conditional residuals of a GLMM, with estimated random-effect modes, is an extension that the catalogue already flags.

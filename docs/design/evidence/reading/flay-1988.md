## Claims checked

03-execution-plan.md lists the paper 'for the smoking-prevention trial'. E/tables/q8_datasets.csv describes mixor::SmokingPrevention (TVSFP) as 1,600 students with school and class identifiers, school-level 2 × 2 randomisation (classroom curriculum, television) and a pre-test; reports.json (research:declarations-data) asks for the randomisation unit and the school and class counts before the data serve as the cluster-randomised vignette. The outcome paper, read in full, is the fuller source for these facts (flay-1995.md).

1. What the paper covers. **Confirmed.** It sets out the theory behind the project (mass-media effects, the social-influences approach to prevention, self-management and social support for cessation) and a model for developing school-based mass-media trials (abstract, p. 585).
2. Randomisation unit and design. **Confirmed** (p. 586 and Figure 1, p. 587). The project assigned 47 public schools in six school districts of Los Angeles and San Diego counties, with about 7,000 seventh-grade students in 340 classrooms, to five experimental conditions using the randomisation procedure of Graham et al., chosen to maximise pretest comparability. The conditions were a social-resistance classroom curriculum with broadcast television segments, the curriculum alone, and television alone with classroom encouragement to watch, beside two controls: measurement-only schools and an information-based attention-control curriculum. All conditions involved families through homework assignments. The classroom-only and control conditions were replicated in San Diego, outside the broadcast area, to guard against contamination by the television programme. Figure 1 gives seven schools per condition in Los Angeles and six in San Diego. The authors describe the whole as two experimental studies within an overall quasi-experimental design.
3. School and class counts of the trial. **Confirmed** at the level of the whole trial: 47 schools and 340 classrooms. The paper does not describe the subset of 1,600 students in mixor.

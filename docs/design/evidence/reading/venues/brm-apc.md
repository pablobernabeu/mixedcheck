# Behavior Research Methods: publication fee

**Page read.** Springer Nature Link, 'How to publish with us | Behavior Research Methods', section 'Fees and funding' (https://link.springer.com/journal/13428/how-to-publish-with-us), read 21 November 2026.

**Fact.** BRM is a hybrid journal. Authors who publish open access pay an article processing charge, set by the date of acceptance; on 21 November 2026 it was £2,690 (US$4,090; €3,090). Publication under the subscription model carries no charge.

**For the record.** Under Oxford's Springer Nature deal, Oxford corresponding authors may publish open access in most hybrid Springer journals at the university's expense (oxford-oa-agreements.md), which would cover this charge if BRM is among the titles included.

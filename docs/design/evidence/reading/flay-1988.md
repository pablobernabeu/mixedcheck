## Claims checked

03-execution-plan.md lists the paper 'for the smoking-prevention trial'. E/tables/q8_datasets.csv describes mixor::SmokingPrevention (TVSFP) as 1,600 students with school and class identifiers, school-level 2 × 2 randomisation (classroom curriculum, television) and a pre-test; reports.json (research:declarations-data) asks for the randomisation unit and the school and class counts before the data serve as the cluster-randomised vignette.

1. What Flay et al. (1988) covers. **Recorded from the abstract.** The paper presents the theory and the programme-development process of the project (mass-media effects, the social-influences approach to prevention, self-management and social support for cessation) and a model for developing school-based mass-media trials. The abstract does not describe the trial's randomisation or its numbers of schools, classes or students, so this paper is unlikely to be the source for those facts.
2. Randomisation unit. **Supported by the outcome paper's abstract.** Schools were randomly assigned to conditions in a '2 × 2 plus I' design comparing classroom delivery, television delivery and both, with 'treatment as usual' and an attention control as control groups; students were surveyed twice in grade 7 and once in grades 8 and 9 (Flay et al., 1995, abstract). The trial therefore had a further arm beyond the 2 × 2 that q8_datasets.csv describes; the dataset in mixor holds the 2 × 2 part.
3. School and class counts. **Not confirmed.** Neither abstract gives them.
